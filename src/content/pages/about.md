## What Fonti is

Fonti is a free tool for turning the words you type into styled text: bold, cursive, gothic, bubble letters, small caps, upside-down text and many more. There is nothing to install, no account to make, and no limit on how often you use it. Type once, tap Copy on the style you like, and paste it into a bio, a caption, a chat or a game name.

## How it works

A font generator does not really generate fonts. Unicode, the standard that lets every phone and computer agree on what text is, includes whole alphabets that happen to look stylish: mathematical script, double-struck letters, circled letters, fullwidth letters. Fonti looks up the matching character for each letter you type and swaps it in.

That is why the result is ordinary text. It survives copy and paste, and it works in places where no font menu exists. It also happens entirely in your browser, so what you type never leaves your device. You can read the full explanation on the [homepage](/).

## Why Fonti is built differently

Most font generator sites give you a long list of styles and promise that everything works everywhere. It does not, and finding that out after you have saved a username is a bad moment. So Fonti is built around telling you the truth.

- **Every style is labelled with where it breaks.** That means missing letters, characters that cost two of your character count, styles that turn into empty boxes on older phones, and fields that reject them outright.
- **Names and handles are treated as separate things.** Instagram, Discord and TikTok all let you style a display name while keeping the @handle plain. Our guides show you which field is which before you paste.
- **We show real screens.** Where a guide includes a screenshot, it is a capture of styled text pasted into the app the guide is about, including the cases where the app refused it.
- **We do not invent numbers.** You will not find made-up engagement percentages here. If something has not been measured, we say so.
- **We talk about accessibility.** Screen readers handle styled text badly, so our guides tell you what to keep in plain characters.

## Keeping the guides accurate

Platform rules change, and a guide that was right last year can be wrong today. We test styles by pasting them into the apps and fields they are meant for, and when we learn that something is out of date, we correct the page instead of leaving it up. If you spot a mistake, please [tell us](/contact/). A short note with the page and the sentence is enough.
