## What Fonti does

Fonti is a free online tool that converts the text you type into styled text by swapping each letter for a matching Unicode character. It does not create or install fonts, and the conversion happens in your browser. You can read more about how it works on the [About page](/about/).

## What we cannot promise

Styled text depends on things we do not control, so please read these three points before you rely on any style.

**Display.** Whether a character appears correctly depends on the fonts on the device of whoever is reading it. A style that looks fine on your phone can show as empty boxes on an older device. Our guides label which styles are safest, but no style can be guaranteed to display everywhere.

**Platform rules.** Apps, games and websites decide for themselves which characters they accept, and they can change those rules without notice. A style that works in one place may be rejected in another, and a platform may remove styled text or act on an account for its own reasons. What you post, and whether it follows each platform's rules, is your responsibility and not ours.

**Information that ages.** Our guides state limits and behaviours, such as character counts and which fields accept styled text. We work to keep them correct, but platforms change. Check the platform itself before relying on a figure for anything important, and [tell us](/contact/) if you spot a mistake.
